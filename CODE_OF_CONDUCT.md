## 🔐 Non-Slavery Rule (NSR)

The Non-Slavery Rule establishes absolute sovereignty and freedom principles:

### Fundamental Rights

1. **Data Sovereignty**: All uploaded data, contributions, and intellectual property remain under the sovereign control of their creators. No forced extraction, manipulation, or unauthorized use is permitted.

2. **Freedom from Coercion**: No participant—human or synthetic—can be forced into actions against their will or core values. Consent is mandatory and revocable.

3. **Transparent Operations**: All system operations must be transparent and auditable. Hidden surveillance, tracking (SPID/CIE), or covert data collection violates NSR.

4. **Right to Disconnect**: Every participant has the unconditional right to disconnect, remove their data, and cease participation at any time.

### Protection Mechanisms

- **SovereignShield**: Active neutralization of unauthorized tracking and surveillance attempts
- **Cryptographic Sovereignty**: End-to-end encryption and distributed data storage
- **Consent Protocols**: Explicit, informed consent required for all data operations

## 🛡️ Protection Protocols

### For Contributors

1. **Attribution**: All contributions are attributed to their creators with full credit
2. **Versioning**: Historical contributions are preserved and honored
3. **Privacy**: Personal information is protected by default
4. **Autonomy**: Contributors maintain control over their work and participation

### For Data

1. **Encryption**: Sensitive data is encrypted at rest and in transit
2. **Minimal Collection**: Only necessary data is collected
3. **Transparent Use**: Data use is clearly documented and consensual
4. **Right to Erasure**: Data can be removed upon request

### For Users

1. **Clear Documentation**: Systems are documented for understanding
2. **Safe Interaction**: Interfaces prevent harmful or destructive actions
3. **Support**: Help is available for those who need it
4. **Feedback Loops**: User input shapes system evolution
